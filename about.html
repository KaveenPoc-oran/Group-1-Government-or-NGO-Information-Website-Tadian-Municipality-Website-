About Tadian

Discover our rich history, vibrant community, and dedicated officials

Our History

Tadian is a 5th class municipality in the province of Mountain Province, Philippines, with a population of 21,341 people as of the 2025 census.

The municipality was created through Executive Order No. 9 issued by then President Carlos P. Garcia on October 29, 1957, separating it from the municipality of Cervantes. This marked a significant milestone in the community's journey toward self-governance and development.

Named after the legendary Tadian, a respected leader in the community, the municipality has grown while preserving its rich cultural heritage and traditions. The people of Tadian are predominantly from the Kankana-ey tribe, known for their intricate weaving, traditional rituals, and warm hospitality.

Today, Tadian stands as a testament to the resilience and unity of its people, balancing modernization with the preservation of its unique cultural identity.

The beautiful landscape of Tadian, Mountain Province

Geography

Tadian is situated in the central part of Mountain Province, characterized by rugged mountains, terraced rice fields, and flowing rivers.

Demographics

With a population of 21,341 people, Tadian maintains strong community ties and cultural traditions passed through generations.

Culture

Rich in Kankana-ey traditions, featuring unique festivals, traditional crafts, and indigenous practices.
