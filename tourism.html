Tourism & Culture

Discover the natural beauty and rich heritage of Tadian

Welcome to Paradise

Tadian offers breathtaking landscapes, rich cultural experiences, and warm hospitality that will make your visit unforgettable. From majestic mountains to cultural festivals, there's something for every traveler.

Popular Attractions

Beautiful mountain ranges perfect for hiking and nature photography

Mountain Valley

Picturesque valley with stunning views and peaceful atmosphere

Cultural Heritage

Lang-ay Festival

The annual Lang-ay Festival is Tadian's biggest cultural celebration, featuring traditional dances, indigenous games, and community feasts. "Lang-ay" means sharing and celebration in the local dialect.

Traditional Dances Native Cuisine Cultural Shows

Traditional Weaving

Tadian is renowned for its intricate traditional weaving patterns. Local artisans create beautiful textiles using age-old techniques passed down through generations.

Travel Information

Getting Here

From Baguio City, take a bus to Bontoc (6 hours), then a jeepney to Tadian (1 hour).

Accommodation

Various homestays and inns available. Book in advance during festival seasons.

Local Cuisine

Try authentic Kankana-ey dishes like pinikpikan, etag, and native coffee.
